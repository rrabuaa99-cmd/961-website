Ordering

Choose your size, add the piece to your bag and check out on the site. You can pay cash on delivery or with Whish Money. If you prefer to chat, you can also send your order on WhatsApp.

Yes, as long as it has not shipped yet. Message us on WhatsApp at +961 3 179 666 with your order number.

Yes. All prices on the site are in US dollars.

Delivery

Orders arrive within 2 to 4 working days across Lebanon.

Delivery is $4 anywhere in Lebanon.

Yes, we deliver across Lebanon. If your area needs extra time, we will let you know before your order ships.

Payment

Cash on delivery or Whish Money in Lebanon. For orders abroad, we tell you how to pay with your shipping quote.

Choose Whish Money at checkout and enter the name and phone number of your Whish account. When you press Pay, we send a Whish payment request for your order total to that number. Approve it in your Whish app, and we call you to arrange delivery. Your order ships once the payment arrives.

Prices are set in US dollars. To pay cash in Lebanese pounds, ask us on WhatsApp for the rate on the day of delivery.

Exchanges

Yes. Message us within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn and unwashed, with the original packaging.

Inside Lebanon, an exchange costs one delivery fee of $4. We collect the old piece and drop off the new one in the same trip.

We offer exchanges rather than refunds. If a piece arrives damaged or wrong, message us within 48 hours and we will replace it at no cost.

International orders

Yes. Choose Outside Lebanon at checkout and place your order. We contact you with the shipping cost and delivery time before you pay anything.

Place your order with nothing charged. We contact you with the shipping cost and how to pay. Cash on delivery is available in Lebanon only.

Yes. Check out with their name, phone number and address, and add a note. We will contact you about payment and contact them to arrange delivery.
